## Routes that exist and are not in the sitemap

These return a page, but they are not in the 401:

- `/villes` is the city hub. `pages-sitemap` drops every `/villes` path, and `villes-sitemap` only emits the 13 city URLs.
- `/services/applications-mobiles` and `/services/logiciel-sur-mesure` are still generated by `app/services/[slug]/page.tsx`. Their `href` in `lib/content.ts` points the sitemap and the canonical at `/developpement-application-mobile-maroc` and `/developpement-logiciel-sur-mesure-maroc`. The short URLs are not redirected.
- `/case-study`, `/contact/merci`, `/audit/lot`, `/categories/[slug]`, `/dashboard/**`, and `/taches` are noindex. Dashboard and `/taches` are also disallowed in `robots.txt`.

## One conflict

`/recherche` is in `pages-sitemap.xml` and its metadata sets `index: false`. It is listed for indexing and marked noindex on the page.
