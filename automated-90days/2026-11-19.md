## Architecture

Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, TypeScript. Site URL is `https://byteforce.ma` from `lib/site.ts`. `www.byteforce.ma` 301s to the apex in `middleware.ts`.

Routes are file-based under `app/`. Commercial pages come from `app/(money)/[...slug]`. City and country service pages come from `lib/geo/pages.ts` and render at `app/geo-location/[[...slug]]/page.tsx`. Insights, services, réalisations, and villes have their own dynamic routes.

The geo set is generated from `lib/geo/coverage.ts`: 16 services, three countries, ten cities (six in Morocco, Paris and Lyon, Montréal and Toronto). Modifier URLs such as `/prix` and `/agence` are not pages. Middleware 301s them to the country or allowed city. `dynamicParams` is false on the geo route.

## Sitemap and robots

`app/sitemap.xml/route.ts` is an index of:

- `/pages-sitemap.xml`
- `/insights-sitemap.xml`
- `/villes-sitemap.xml`
- `/geo-location-sitemap.xml`
- `/tools-sitemap.xml`

`app/robots.ts` matches the live file in production: allow `/`, disallow dashboard, taches, and cdn-cgi, and point at those sitemaps plus the index.

Canonicals are set per route with `alternates.canonical`, usually the path of that page. The root layout also sets `metadataBase` to the site URL, a title template `%s | BYTE FORCE`, and `hreflang` `fr-MA` and `fr` both pointing at the homepage. Geo, money, insight, service, ville, and realisation pages set their own canonical to their own path.

The layout default robots are index and follow. `/recherche` is noindex. `/audit` is noindex when it has a target query.

## Risks

1. The sitemap index lastmod values are hardcoded (31 October, 12 October, 27 October 2026). The geo sitemap constant in code is 10 November 2026. The live index still shows 12 October for geo. Crawlers can treat those dates as stale.
2. The homepage title in production does not match the default title string in `app/layout.tsx`. Worth confirming which phrase should rank.
3. `fr-MA` and `fr` both canonicalise to the same homepage. That becomes a problem when the France and English phases add real language URLs.
4. Geo pages are one template with the place and service swapped in. Day 3 of the calendar is the right time to judge which combinations are thin. This pass did not sample them.
5. The calendar's own Search Console snapshot (15 August–15 November 2026) was not re-queried today: Morocco 93 clicks / 4,390 impressions, France 0 / 593, United States 0 / 114, Canada 1 / 31. Treat that as the sheet's baseline, not a fresh export.
