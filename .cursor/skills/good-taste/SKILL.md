# Good taste for Byte Force

Preserve ByteForce's existing brand and valuable website sections.

Use AltCreative as visual inspiration rather than a template to copy.

Avoid generic AI-generated design patterns.

Prioritize composition, typography, whitespace, real product visuals, and meaningful interactions.

Preserve existing SEO content, routes, internal links, and conversion flows.

Inspect rendered screenshots and iterate before declaring the redesign complete.

## How to apply that

- Keep the blue studio mark, Public Sans, JetBrains Mono, and the light surface. Do not invent a logo, a purple gradient, or a dark theme for the whole site.
- AltCreative may inform type scale, cropping, rhythm, and how a real screenshot sits in the page. Do not copy its sections, palette, or fake product UI.
- Prefer a hairline, a real capture, or a change in scale over another identical rounded card.
- Do not delete a section because a reference does not have it. If the words, the link, or the conversion path matter, restyle them.
- `/contact` stays the main action. The audit stays a working form, not a picture of a form.
- A redesign is unfinished until the changed page has been opened at a desktop width and a phone width and the obvious collisions are fixed.
