# byteforce.ma Landing Page Audit

Oct 5, 2026 · @Walid

byteforce.ma is a strong product-studio page, but it sells only build-from-scratch work and lacks proof, pricing and maintenance offers. Checked items below are what the page already does; unchecked items are missing or weak. Reviewed from page text and metadata only, so speed, mobile layout and the contact page were not tested.

## 1. Hero section (first 5 seconds)

- [x] A clear headline saying who you help and what you do
- [x] A one-line subheadline with the outcome the client gets
- [x] One primary call to action above the fold
- [ ] A visual that shows real work, not generic stock images

**Finding:** the headline and the Start a project button work well. The hero visual is a network diagram with stats like 99.98% uptime and 12ms latency, which look decorative and may read as invented.

**Fix:** replace the diagram with a real screenshot or short video of Cocoinbox or YourSmile, or label the stats as a sample.

## 2. Services

- [x] Separate, clearly named offers (build-side only)
- [x] Short descriptions of what each offer includes
- [x] The tech stack you work with
- [ ] Who each service is for (startup founder, business owner with an existing site)
- [ ] Bug fixing, audits and rescue of an existing project as a named offer
- [ ] Website maintenance as a named offer

**Finding:** all six services are about building new software. Nothing speaks to someone who already has a product or site that is broken or needs upkeep.

**Fix:** add two cards, Audit and Bug Fixing and Maintenance and Support, each with 3 bullets of what is included and a Get an audit button. Add a one-line audience tag to every card (for example: For founders launching an MVP).

## 3. Proof of competence

- [ ] 3 to 6 case studies with problem, solution and result, ideally with numbers
- [x] Live links or screenshots of real projects
- [ ] Testimonials with real name, photo, company and role
- [ ] Client logos
- [ ] Reviews on external platforms (Google, Clutch, Upwork)

**Finding:** four real products are shown, but only Cocoinbox has a working case-study link. The YourSmile link points back to the same section, and ProcheDeMoi and Custom CRM have none. There are no numbers, quotes or logos anywhere.

**Fix:**

1. Write a one-page case study for each product using problem, solution, stack and result.
2. Add at least one measurable result per case (hours saved, users, load time, bugs fixed).
3. Ask one client for a 2-sentence testimonial with name, role and photo.
4. Create a free Google Business Profile and Clutch listing, then link the reviews.
5. Fix or remove the broken YourSmile link.

## 4. Process

- [x] A simple step-by-step flow (Understand, Design, Build, Launch, Improve)
- [ ] How you communicate (Slack, WhatsApp, weekly updates, a project board)
- [ ] How you handle bugs found after delivery
- [ ] Who owns the code and the accounts at the end

**Finding:** the 5-step flow is clear, but it stops at what you do and never says how the client experiences the work or what happens at handover.

**Fix:** add a short line under the process: weekly demo, shared board, reply within one business day, and a 30-day free bug-fix window after launch. Add a line stating the client owns the code, repositories and hosting accounts at the end.

## 5. Maintenance and support

- [ ] Plans or packages: monitoring, backups, security updates, uptime checks, content edits
- [ ] Response times or SLA
- [ ] A monthly retainer or pay-as-you-go option
- [ ] An emergency or my-site-is-down contact path

**Finding:** this is the biggest gap. The only trace is the word Improve in the process, and the page has no maintenance offer at all.

**Fix:** add a Maintenance and Support section with three tiers:

| Plan | Includes | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care | Uptime monitoring, weekly backups, security updates | 2 business days |
| Care Plus | Care, plus 5 hours per month of fixes and content edits | 1 business day |
| Priority | Care Plus, plus performance tuning and a monthly report | 4 hours for critical issues |

Show a Site is down? button linking to WhatsApp or a dedicated support email. The tiers and response times are suggestions; set them to what you can actually deliver.

## 6. Pricing and expectations

- [ ] Starting prices, ranges or package tiers
- [ ] What affects the price
- [ ] Typical timelines
- [ ] A free consultation or audit offer to lower the barrier

**Finding:** there is no pricing, timeline or free offer anywhere on the page, so every visitor has to guess before contacting you.

**Fix:**

1. Show starting prices or ranges (for example: MVP from X MAD, audit from Y MAD).
2. Add one line listing what moves the price: scope, integrations, number of user roles.
3. Add typical timelines: audit in 3 to 5 days, MVP in 6 to 10 weeks.
4. Offer a free 30-minute call or a free quick audit of the visitor's site as the main hook.

## 7. Trust and credibility

- [ ] An About section with real team photos, names and experience
- [ ] Years in business, number of projects delivered
- [x] Business details: location, registered company name
- [ ] NDA and confidentiality assurance
- [ ] Security practices (code reviews, staging environments, backups)

**Finding:** the footer shows ByteForce Studio SARL and Casablanca, which helps. The menu links to About and Insights, but I did not find either section in the page content, so those links may be dead. No person is visible behind the agency.

**Fix:**

1. Build the About section: founder name and photo, a 3-line story, years of experience, and the number of products shipped.
2. Build the Insights section with 2 or 3 short articles, or remove it from the menu until it exists.
3. Add a trust strip with three promises: NDA on request, staging environment before every release, client owns the code.
4. Add a street address or a map pin for the Casablanca office.

## 8. Contact and conversion

- [ ] A short contact form (name, email, project type, budget range, message) *contact page not opened*
- [ ] Multiple contact options: email, WhatsApp, calendar booking link
- [x] A CTA repeated after each major section, not only at the bottom
- [ ] A promise on reply time
- [x] A professional business email, not only a Gmail address (but two different ones are shown)

**Finding:** the call to action appears in many places, which is good. The page shows two different emails (contact@byteforce.ma and studio@byteforce.io), and I saw no WhatsApp number, booking link or reply-time promise. I did not open the contact page itself, so the form fields are unchecked.

**Fix:**

1. Use one email address on one domain everywhere (the .ma one) and redirect the other.
2. Add a WhatsApp button, which is the fastest channel for Moroccan clients.
3. Add a Cal.com or Calendly booking link next to Start a project.
4. Add Reply within 24 hours under the form, and ask for project type and budget range in the form.

## 9. FAQ

- [ ] What if I already have code written by someone else?
- [ ] Can you work with my existing stack?
- [ ] Do you offer ongoing support after launch?
- [ ] How do payments work (deposit, milestones)?
- [ ] What happens if the scope changes?

**Finding:** the page has no FAQ.

**Fix:** add 6 to 8 questions near the bottom, starting with the five above. These also answer the exact worries of clients who need bug fixes or maintenance, and they add searchable text for Google.

## 10. Technical quality of the page itself

- [ ] Loads fast (under 3 seconds, good Lighthouse score) *not tested*
- [ ] Fully responsive on mobile *not tested*
- [ ] No broken links, images or console errors (YourSmile case-study link is a placeholder)
- [x] HTTPS and a clean, consistent design
- [ ] Accessible contrast and readable fonts *not tested*
- [x] Basic SEO: title, meta description, Open Graph preview, canonical, robots
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms linked in the footer

**Finding:** the SEO metadata is complete, and the design language is consistent. Gaps are the meta description being in English while the social preview is in French, a footer dated 2025, and no legal pages. The site claims English, French and Arabic delivery, but I saw no language switch.

**Fix:**

1. Run Lighthouse and PageSpeed Insights on mobile and fix anything under 90, since a dev agency is judged by its own speed.
2. Make the meta description and social description the same language, or add a French version of the site (most local clients search in French).
3. Update the footer year to 2026, or generate it automatically.
4. Add Privacy Policy and Terms pages and link them in the footer.
5. Check that every section icon matches its card (a few look mismatched, such as palette for SaaS and smartphone for AI).
6. Add a proper About page and sitemap.xml so Google can index the full site.

## 11. Red flags that make clients leave

| Red flag | On byteforce.ma? | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vague claims with no specifics | Partly | Phrases like real operational problems carry no numbers |
| No portfolio or only mockups | No | Four real products shown |
| No pricing or process information | Partly | Process is shown, pricing is not |
| Dead links, placeholder text or a slow, broken site | Partly | YourSmile case-study link, possibly About and Insights |
| No visible human behind the agency | Yes | No team names or photos |

**Fix:** the top two to close are the missing human face (About section with photo) and the dead links, since both are quick and remove the strongest trust blockers.

Do fixes 1 to 4 first: they close the biggest gaps for clients who need bug fixes, maintenance or proof before they hire.
